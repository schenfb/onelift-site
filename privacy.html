Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Soliday is built to respect your privacy. The short version: your training data stays on your phone, and the only thing we collect is anonymous, non-identifying usage statistics to help us improve the app.

Personal information

We don't collect any. Soliday has no account, no login, and no sign-up. We never ask for or store your name, email, or any information that identifies you.

Your training data

  • Your workouts, weights, and history are stored locally on your device only.
  • We never send your training data to any server. There is no cloud and no backend.
  • You can export your data yourself at any time from within the app.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data.

Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how the app is used and make it better, Soliday collects anonymous, aggregate usage events — for example, that the app was opened or that a workout was completed — using PostHog, a first-party product-analytics tool (data hosted in the United States).

  • These events are tied only to a random, anonymous identifier — not to you, and not to your name, email, or device advertising ID.
  • We do not collect the contents of your training log this way.
  • This data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you enable them, Soliday schedules local notifications (rest-timer alerts and training reminders) directly on your device. These are never sent through a server.

Sharing

Share cards (your workout summary images) are generated on your device and are only shared when you choose to share them, through your own device's share sheet.

Advertising & tracking

Soliday contains no ads and no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is needed. The only analytics is the anonymous, first-party usage measurement described above.

Children

Soliday does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@onelift.org.
