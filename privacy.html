Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Onelift is built to respect your privacy. The short version: we don't collect your data. Everything you log stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Onelift has no account, no login, and no sign-up. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server.

Your training data

  • Your workouts, weights, and history are stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not send this data anywhere. There is no cloud, no backend, no analytics on your activity.
  • You can export your data yourself at any time from within the app.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data.

Notifications

If you enable them, Onelift schedules local notifications (rest-timer alerts and training reminders) directly on your device. These are never sent through a server.

Sharing

Share cards (your workout summary images) are generated on your device and are only shared when you choose to share them, through your own device's share sheet.

Third parties & tracking

Onelift contains no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

Children

Onelift does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@onelift.org.
